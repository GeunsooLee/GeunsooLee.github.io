Article 3 (Idle Reward)

Once a day, you can receive coins for the time you were away from the App (up to 8 hours). The reward amount is calculated based on your earning speed in recent fishing runs. In the mobile version, you can get double the reward by watching an ad, and if you purchase "Remove Ads," you always get double with no ads. The Steam version always gives double.
