Article 1 (Service)

The App is a game in which you place food and items in the yard of a Korean house, cats and animal guests come to visit, and you play with them and take photos to fill your Cat Book. You can also take the cats onto your home screen on your phone, or onto your desktop on PC. There is no sign-up or login, and the game runs on your device. All cats and animals in the App are fictional, illustrated characters (a cat you create from your own photo with "Make My Cat" is also drawn only as an illustration).
