Meow Yard Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Meow Yard (the "App") is a cat and animal collection game made by the developer DGSTUDIO (the "Developer"). It runs on mobile devices (iPhone/iPad/Android) and on PC (Steam — Windows/macOS). This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Personal information collected by the Developer: None

  • The App has no sign-up or login, and the Developer does not operate any servers.
  • The Developer does not collect or store information such as your name, email, phone number, location, or contacts.
  • The App does not use analytics tools. Ads, payments, cloud saves, and stream chat are handled by the external services listed below (Section 6).

2. Information stored on your device and in your own account

  • Your yard layout, visiting cats and animals, affection, the names you give them, photo cards, coins and treats, purchased products, and sound, vibration, and language settings are stored on your device.
  • "Photos" in the App are pictures taken of the artwork on the game screen; the App does not use the camera. Photo cards and "Yard Show-off Cards" are passed to your device's share screen only when you tap Share (on PC, the folder where the photos are saved is opened instead).
  • In "Make My Cat," the one photo you choose yourself is used only on your device to pick fur colors and patterns; it is not saved or sent anywhere. You choose the photo with your operating system's photo picker, and the App does not request permission to access your entire photo library.
  • This information is not sent to the Developer. It is deleted when you delete the App or clear the App's data.
  • Depending on your device's backup settings, it may be included in an iCloud backup (your own Apple account) or Android Auto Backup (your own Google account).
  • Cloud save: So you can continue after changing devices, your Cat Book and affection, names, photo slots (excluding images), coins and treats, purchased items and decorations, and purchase status are saved to your own iCloud (iPhone/iPad), to Saved Games in Google Play Games (Android), or to Steam Cloud (PC). This storage belongs to your own account, and the Developer does not access or receive it.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration (Android): Used for short vibrations, such as a purring vibration when you pet a cat.
  • Internet: Used to load ads, process payments, and for cloud saves (Section 2) and Stream mode (Section 5).
  • Display over other apps (Android, only when you turn on "Cats on screen"): Lets the cats walk around on top of your home screen. While the cats are out, a notification is also shown, and you can call them back from it at any time. The App does not read the content of other apps' screens.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iPhone/iPad): The App asks once for permission to use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads. The game and ad rewards are the same even if you do not allow it.
  • The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

4. PC (Steam) desktop mode

  • So that the cats can stand on top of other programs' windows or desktop icons, the App checks only the position and size of the windows visible on screen and the positions of desktop icons, on your device. It does not read window contents, window titles, or file names, and this information is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • On macOS, if you turn on "Climb onto desktop icons," the App asks once for permission to control Finder so it can check the icon positions. Other features work as usual even if you do not allow it.
  • The mouse cursor position is used only on your device so the cats can follow the cursor, and the App does not read keyboard input.

5. Stream mode (PC, only when you turn it on)

  • When you enter a Twitch channel name and turn on Stream mode, the App only reads that channel's public chat, without logging in. It does not post anything in the chat.
  • Viewers' display names and commands (such as !treat) are only shown briefly on screen along with the cats' reactions; they are not saved or sent to the Developer. Only the channel name you entered is stored on your device.

6. Information processed by external services

The services below process information under their own privacy policies, and the Developer does not receive or store that information.

  • Google AdMob (ads, mobile): Shows ads when you tap to watch an ad, and when you launch the App or reopen it after a while. Advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, and IDFA if you allowed it), IP address, device and app information, and ad view and click information may be processed. In regions where consent is required, such as Europe, you will be asked for consent first. (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • App Store / Google Play / Steam (payments): Process payment and purchase verification information when you buy products and additional content. Payment method information such as card details is processed by Apple, Google, and Valve, and the Developer does not receive it.
  • Apple iCloud / Google Play Games Services / Steam (cloud save, achievements): Keep the saved data described in Section 2 and your achievements in your own account. (apple.com/legal/privacy · policies.google.com/privacy · store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement)
  • Twitch (Stream mode, only when you turn it on): The App connects to Twitch's servers to receive the public chat, and connection information such as your IP address may be passed to Twitch at that time. (twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-notice)

7. Children's personal information

The Developer does not directly collect anyone's personal information. Information processed by the ad provider is as described in Section 6.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and announce it with an App update.
