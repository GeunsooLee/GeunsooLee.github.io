Puzzle Heaven Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Puzzle Heaven (the "App") is a collection of puzzle games made by the developer DGSTUDIO (the "Developer"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. This policy is a translation of the Korean original; if the two differ, the Korean version prevails to the extent permitted by law.

1. Personal information collected by the Developer: none

· The App has no sign-up or login, and the Developer runs no servers.

· The Developer does not collect or keep information such as your name, email, phone number, location or contacts.

· The App uses no analytics tools. Ads, payments and cloud save are handled by the external services below (section 6).

2. Photos

· In games with a photo pattern feature, when you tap "Make from photo", your phone's system photo picker opens and only the one photo you choose is passed to the App. The App does not access your whole photo library (on iPhone and iPad it does not request photo library access).

· The chosen photo is converted on your device into a pattern for that game (color data for each cell, the order of placement, etc.). The original photo file is not stored, and any temporary file created for the conversion is deleted right away.

· In the game where you wipe a photo clean to reveal it ("Rinsify"), the photo itself is the underlying picture, so a reduced copy of the area you chose (640 pixels on the long side) is kept in the App's on-device storage. This copy is not uploaded to cloud save either.

· In the drone light show game ("Skylumi"), the background is removed so that only the subject of the photo remains. This is done by Apple Vision (iPhone and iPad) or Google ML Kit (Android), which run on your device, and the photo never leaves your device.

· Photos and patterns are never sent to the Developer or to any third party. Original photos are never uploaded anywhere; pattern data may be kept only in your own account through the cloud save described in section 3.

· If you tap "Save photo" on the completion screen of some games, one image of the completed screen is added to your phone's photo album. On iPhone and iPad you are asked once for "Add Photos" permission (the App does not read your album).

3. Information stored on your phone and in your own account

· Coins and purchased items shared by all games, each game's progress (cleared levels and stars, puzzles in progress, upgrades, collections, etc.), patterns made from photos, and sound, music, vibration and language settings are stored on your phone.

· Idle games (fishing and bees) also store the time you last played and your recent income rate so that rewards for the time you were away can be calculated.

· This information is not sent to the Developer. It is deleted when you delete the App or clear the App's data in your phone's settings. You can also delete patterns one by one from each game's "My Photos" list.

· Depending on your phone's backup settings, it may be included in an iCloud backup (your own Apple account) or Android Auto Backup (your own Google account).

· Cloud save: so you can continue on a new phone, coins and purchase status, each game's progress, and patterns made from photos are saved to your own iCloud (iPhone and iPad) or to Google Play Games saved games (Android). Original photos, settings and puzzles in progress are not uploaded. This storage belongs to your own account, and the Developer cannot access or receive it. Data moves only between devices of the same kind on the same account, and the cloud copy remains even if you delete the App. You can turn it off or delete it in your iCloud settings or Google Play Games settings.

4. Permissions and sensors

· Vibration (Android): used for short vibrations at moments such as placing a puzzle piece or catching a big fish. You can turn it off in each game's settings.

· Internet and network status: used to load ads, process payments, for cloud save (section 3), and to download the background removal model (Android).

· Add Photos (iPhone and iPad): used only when you tap "Save photo" (section 2).

· App Tracking Transparency (iPhone and iPad): the App asks once for permission to use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads. The games and ad rewards are the same whether or not you allow it.

· Motion sensor: if you turn on "Tilt view" in some games, the screen shifts slightly with the tilt of your phone. This value is used only for the on-screen effect and is neither stored nor sent.

· The App does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

5. Children's information

The Developer does not directly collect anyone's personal information. Information processed by the ad provider is described in section 6.

6. Information processed by external services

The services below process information under their own privacy policies; the Developer does not receive or keep that information.

· Google AdMob (ads): shows ads when you tap a rewarded ad and when you move on from the results screen after finishing a round. Advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, and IDFA if you allowed it), IP address, device and app information, and ad view and click information may be processed. In regions that require consent, such as Europe, you are asked for consent first. (policies.google.com/privacy)

· App Store / Google Play (payments): process payment and purchase verification information when you buy coins or items. Payment method details such as card information are handled by Apple or Google, and the Developer does not receive them.

· Apple iCloud / Google Play Games Services (cloud save): keep the saved data described in section 3 in your own account. Play Games signs you in with your Play Games profile. (apple.com/legal/privacy · policies.google.com/privacy)

· Google ML Kit (Android, background removal): the first time you use it, a model that runs on your device is downloaded through Google Play services. Photos are processed only on your device. (developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms)

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and announce it with an App update.
