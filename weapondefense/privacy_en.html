Weapon Defense Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Weapon Defense (무기디펜스, the "App") is a defense game made by the developer DGSTUDIO (the "Developer"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. This policy is a translation of the Korean original; if the two differ, the Korean version prevails to the extent permitted by law.

1. Personal information collected by the Developer: none

· The App has no sign-up, and the Developer runs no servers. Cloud save uses your own Apple account (iCloud) or Google Play Games account (sections 2 and 5).

· The Developer does not collect or keep information such as your name, email, phone number, location or contacts.

· The App uses no analytics tools. Ads and payments are handled by the external services below (section 5).

2. Information stored on your device

· Your account level and passives, coins, unlocked cards, codex, decks, stage records, the run in progress, ad reward counts, and sound, vibration, game speed and language settings are stored only on your device.

· This information is not sent to the Developer. It is deleted when you delete the App or tap "Erase All Data" in Settings (the language setting stays).

· Cloud save: a copy of your account level and passives, coins, unlocked cards, codex, purchase records, stage records and ad reward counts is kept in your own iCloud (iPhone and iPad — iCloud key-value storage) or Google Play Games "Saved Games" (Android), so you can continue after changing devices or reinstalling. This copy is not sent to the Developer either, and tapping "Erase All Data" in Settings also empties the cloud copy.

· On iPhone and iPad, depending on your device's backup settings, it may also be included in iCloud Backup. On Android it is excluded from device backups.

3. Permissions

· Internet: used to load ads, process payments and exchange the cloud save.

· App Tracking Transparency (iPhone and iPad): the App may ask for permission to use the advertising ID (IDFA) for personalized ads. The game and ad rewards are the same whether or not you allow it.

· The App does not request location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone permissions.

4. Children's personal information

The Developer does not directly collect anyone's personal information. Information processed by the ad provider is described in section 5.

5. Information processed by external services

The services below process information under their own privacy policies; the Developer does not receive or keep that information.

· Google AdMob (ads): shows ads when you tap "Watch Ad: Double Coins" or "Watch Ad: Free Coins", and when you open the App or return to it after a while. The advertising ID (IDFA if allowed), IP address, device and app information, and ad view and click information may be processed. In regions that require consent, such as the EU, you are asked first, and you can change your choice at any time under "Ad Consent" in Settings. (policies.google.com/privacy)

· App Store / Google Play (payments): process payment and purchase confirmation when you buy coins. Payment details such as card information are handled by Apple or Google and not received by the Developer. (apple.com/legal/privacy · policies.google.com/privacy)

· iCloud / Google Play Games (cloud save): keep the game progress copy described in section 2 in your own account. On Android, signing in to Google Play Games may be required; you can play without signing in (only cloud save is off). (apple.com/legal/privacy · policies.google.com/privacy)

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date will be updated and the change announced with an App update.
