Dominify Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Dominify (the "App") is a puzzle game made by the developer DGSTUDIO (the "Developer"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. This policy is a translation of the Korean original; if the two differ, the Korean version prevails to the extent permitted by law.

1. Personal information collected by the Developer: none

· The App has no sign-up, and the Developer runs no servers.

· The Developer does not collect or keep information such as your name, email, phone number, location or contacts.

· The App uses no analytics tools. Ads, payments and cloud save are handled by the external services below (section 6).

2. Photos

· When you tap "Make from photo", your phone's system photo picker opens and only the one photo you choose is passed to the App. The App does not access your whole photo library (on iPhone and iPad it does not request photo library access).

· The chosen photo is converted on your device into domino board data (the number of pips in each cell, the solution layout of the dominoes and the color of each cell). The original photo is not stored, and any temporary copy made for the conversion is deleted right away.

· Photos and board data are never sent to the Developer or to any third party, and original photos are never uploaded anywhere. A saved photo puzzle contains only this board data and is kept only on your device and in the cloud save of your own account (section 3).

3. Information stored on your phone

· Puzzles made from photos (board data), cleared levels and stars, puzzles in progress, coins, free tickets, hints and checks, purchased items, and sound, vibration and language settings are stored on your phone.

· This information is not sent to the Developer. It is deleted when you delete the App or clear the App's data in your phone's settings. You can also delete photo puzzles one by one from the "My Photos" list.

· Cloud save: so you can keep playing after changing phones, the App saves your solved levels and stars, coins, free tickets, hints and checks, purchase status, and the board data of puzzles made from photos to your own iCloud (iPhone, iPad) or to Saved Games in Google Play Games (Android). Original photos, sound, vibration and language settings, and boards in progress are not uploaded. This storage belongs to your own account, and the Developer cannot access or receive it. Data moves only between devices of the same kind signed in to the same account, and the cloud copy remains even if you delete the App. You can turn it off or delete it in your iCloud settings or Google Play Games settings.

· Depending on your phone's backup settings, the App's data may also be included in an iCloud backup (your own Apple account) or Android Auto Backup (your own Google account). These backups also belong to your own account, and the Developer cannot access or receive them.

4. Permissions

· Vibration (Android): used for short vibrations when you place dominoes and during the domino toppling animation.

· Internet: used to load ads, process payments and for cloud save (section 3).

· App Tracking Transparency (iPhone and iPad): the App asks once for permission to use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads. The game and ad rewards are the same whether or not you allow it.

· The App does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

5. Children's information

The Developer does not directly collect anyone's personal information. Information processed by the ad provider is described in section 6.

6. Information processed by external services

The services below process information under their own privacy policies; the Developer does not receive or keep that information.

· Google AdMob (ads): shows an opening ad when you open the App or come back to it after 1 hour or more, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch (to get coins, to double the coins for completing a puzzle, or for a helper when you are stuck). Advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, and IDFA if you allowed it), IP address, device and app information, and ad view and click information may be processed. In regions that require consent, such as Europe, you are first asked for consent through Google's consent form (UMP). The game and ad rewards are the same even if you do not consent. (policies.google.com/privacy)

· App Store / Google Play (payments): process payment and purchase verification information when you buy coins or items, and keep the purchase record of permanent items in your store account for "Restore Purchases". Payment method details such as card information are handled by Apple or Google, and the Developer does not receive them. (apple.com/legal/privacy · policies.google.com/privacy)

· Apple iCloud / Google Play Games Services (cloud save): keep the saved data in section 3 in your own account. On Android, you sign in with your Play Games profile. (apple.com/legal/privacy · policies.google.com/privacy)

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and announce it with an App update.
